# Research Interactive Presentations (RIP)

A RIP is a scroll-driven companion to a hub research briefing. The briefing argues; the RIP shows the
mechanism. First one: `Research/context-vs-mcp/story/`.

## Shape
- Lives at `Research/<slug>/story/` and is linked from the briefing (a line under the cover's as-of note).
- `STORYBOARD.md` is the source of truth: one scene per briefing section, each with a number, an image beat,
  a line, and an honesty line where something is modelled or the author's own read.
- The cover image is the stage. Find the mapping from the banner's metaphor to the article (the
  context-vs-mcp table is the model), then cut the cover into panes so scenes can take it apart.
- A sticky canvas stage, with narration cards scrolling past it. Narration is real text; the canvas is `aria-hidden`.
- At least the hardest scenes get a knob (toggle, slider or drag) whose effect is visible at once, and every
  knob also works from the narration card, so phones and keyboards aren't left out.

## Build and validate (thinkinfosec-visuals skill)
RIPs are built by the `thinkinfosec-visuals` skill; its `docs/rip.md` is the full guide.

| File | Role |
|---|---|
| `rip.json` | The spec: narration, hero, hub chrome, art files, the `briefing` link. Edit this |
| `scenes.js` | One draw function per scene, plus knob wiring. Edit this |
| `index.html`, `story.css`, `rip-runtime.js`, `rip-art.js`, `theme-boot.js` | Generated by `rip.mjs build`; never edit |
| `atlas*.webp`, `lead*.webp`, `panes.json` | The cover cut into panes, from `build/panes.py` |

```
node ~/.claude/skills/thinkinfosec-visuals/scripts/rip.mjs build Research/<slug>/story/rip.json
node ~/.claude/skills/thinkinfosec-visuals/scripts/rip.mjs validate Research/<slug>/story --shots <dir>
```

It plays in steps: every card is a step the page snaps to, and the stage plays that step to its end frame, so any scroll lands on a finished frame. `validate` must pass (Edge, from file:// and over http, at three desktop and two phone sizes, with real wheel, touch and keyboard input) before merging.

## Rules
- Strict CSP meta: `'self'` only, no third-party scripts, fonts or analytics.
- Dark by default, light via the toggle; hub tokens; 4.5:1 contrast, and no pale blue on blue.
- Every number comes from the briefing, with its source in the card. Vendor numbers say so.
- No telemetry-style status strings.
- `prefers-reduced-motion`: each step shows its end frame at once; time-based motion stops.
- The story ends on its final sentence, with the "Read the full briefing" link under it (the build adds it).
- Run `validate`, then look at the 1440×900 and 390×844 shots, before merging.
