# Kernel-Access Redesigned

## Style Prompt

A dark briefing frame for eight diagrams that already exist. Navy field, glass cards, teal for structure that shipped, magenta for risk, conditions, and unfinished previews. The voice is a research account: a serif states the claim, a mono states the kernel fact. Motion reveals the path the diagram already draws. Nothing new is claimed.

## Colors

- `#0D1F3C` — canvas. The navy behind every existing diagram.
- `#16304C` — card fill, lifted just enough to read as a plate on the canvas.
- `#F4F7F6` — ink. Titles and card names.
- `#00BFCF` — structure. Borders, arrows, and stages that shipped. ThinkInfoSec teal.
- `#FF2D78` — risk borders. Ring 0, the outage, private preview, HVCI, conditions. ThinkInfoSec magenta.
- `#FFB4C8` — the same magenta, lifted so small labels clear the card. Borders stay `#FF2D78`.
- `#D5E4E6` — secondary ink on cards. Muted, still light enough to read on `#16304C`.

Neutrals lean cool, toward the teal, never dead gray. No pure black, no pure white.

## Typography

- **Newsreader** — statements. Scene titles and card names. The human account.
- **IBM Plex Mono** — years, tags, paths, and captions. The mechanism.

The tension is the piece: a person explaining ring 0. Serif speaks the claim. Mono speaks the address, the year, and the hook.

## Motion

- Entrances only, until the last scene, which fades out.
- Pipeline scenes travel left to right. The two-philosophy scene enters from opposite sides. The status board enters from four corners.
- Teal draws a path. Magenta marks the step that is still conditional or unfinished.
- Ambient motion is a slow glow drift, finite, never a loop that runs forever.
- Transitions between scenes: a crossfade when the chapter continues, a push when the operating system changes. The same two moves, used for that reason.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not use Inter, Roboto, or a second sans.
- Do not recolor the diagrams into a generic cyan-on-black tech palette. Teal and magenta are the brand, and they already have jobs.
- Do not invent years, product names, or a general-availability date.
- Do not put small type on a solid magenta fill.
- Do not exit a scene’s contents before the transition. The transition is the exit.
